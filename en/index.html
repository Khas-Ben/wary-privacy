Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

In short: Wary does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device, and nowhere else.

1. The principle: everything stays on your device

Wary is a personal budgeting app developed by kablanprivat@gmail.com. It is designed to work entirely offline. The app has no server, no remote database and no user accounts.

Everything you enter — goals, income, budget envelopes, categories, expenses, items, recurring bills and receipt photos — is stored on your phone only, in the app's internal storage. We have no access whatsoever to that data.

2. Data we collect

None. Wary does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The app contains:

  • no analytics or audience measurement tools;
  • no advertising services;
  • no tracking SDKs;
  • no bank or financial connections.

3. Permissions used

Permission Purpose Where does the data go?
📷 Camera Photograph a receipt while adding an expense Saved locally on the device. Never sent off the device.
🖼️ Photo library Import an existing photo of a receipt Used and stored 100% locally.

These permissions are requested only at the moment you use the corresponding feature. You can revoke them at any time from your device settings.

4. Data sharing

Wary never shares data automatically. Sharing can only happen if you take an explicit action:

  • Backup (JSON export) or CSV export from the Settings screen: these files are generated on your device and sent through your system's native share sheet to the destination of your choice. We never receive a copy of these exports.
  • Restore: you can paste the contents of a JSON backup to restore your data; this is processed entirely on your device.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed.
  • You can erase your expenses from Settings → "Reset expenses".
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data (unless you have exported a backup yourself).

6. Children's data

Since Wary collects no data at all, the app does not knowingly collect any data concerning children either.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example when an optional encrypted sync feature is added), the update date at the top of this document will be changed and the new version will be published with the app.
